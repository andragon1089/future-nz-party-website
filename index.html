Keeping Our Doctors Home

What's the Issue?

New Zealand has an ongoing lack of workers in healthcare. In a recent poll, New Zealanders ranked our healthcare system an average of 34/100. With a 38% deficit of residential nurses, healthcare systems are quickly being overwhelmed. New Zealand is undeniably in a healthcare crisis, and changes must be made. Hospitals in Dargaville, Kaitaia, and Kawakawa have over one in three positions empty, and are forced to send critical patients to larger hospitals by ambulance.

New Zealand's government has tried to solve this issue, but has been unsuccessful. $34.2 million dollars has recently been distributed across several nurse training facilities, yet 71% of those nurses move to other countries within three years. What hasn't been addressed is retention, not capacity. Health Minister Simeon Brown has admitted the healthcare system is in crisis, yet no concrete action has been done.

How we'll do it

Our proposed solution tackles these issues with a five-year student debt cancellation. By offering students in medical fields a way to erase what could be the largest financial burden of their life, we give New Zealanders an incentive to stay and work in New Zealand and support our healthcare system. For every year over five years, 20% of their student debt will be cancelled. This can be reduced to three years by working in rural regions. When these new doctors spend their first few years out of university in New Zealand, they're far more likely to settle down and feed tax dollars back into the economy. Their presence will also cut the need for expensive locum doctors, reducing the financial burden this policy introduces.

Scheduling GP visits will be easier and more flexible, Emergency Department wait times will drop significantly, and every Kiwi will have easier, more reliable access to healthcare. Our government's $220 million per year spending on locum doctors will drop, and rural regions will no longer have to ambulance people to larger hospitals. With time, our healthcare system will fully recover, letting you and your family be at ease.
